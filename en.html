ABOUT ME

Who Am I?

I'm Haneen. I love drawing and expressing ideas and feelings through art. I find in drawing a space to explore my imagination, experiment with colors, and turn simple ideas into works that carry different meanings and details.

This website is my personal gallery, where I gather my drawings and my artistic journey and share a part of my world with you.

ABOUT MY ART

My Art Philosophy

I believe art is the purest form of expression, which is why I never limit myself to a single style. My work ranges from intricate black-ink sketching and warm graphite shading to vibrant watercolors and playful handmade crafts.

Precision of ink

Fine lines and richly shaded details that tell a story.

Warmth of graphite

Soft gradients that give the shapes depth and feeling.

Joy of color

Watercolor and warm gradients that make a piece feel playful.

Handmade dimensions

Paper and cups turned into little sculptures you can almost touch.
